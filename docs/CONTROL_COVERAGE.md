Source transports remain disabled unless an operator explicitly configures the account, entitlement, keys, and zero-price grant.

## Qualification boundaries

- Source tests use injected fake transports, including the explicitly granted live branch. They make no real Qloo, model, venue, rights, or other provider request.
- A signed business feed is an integration protocol for an independently provisioned fact source. It does not create an inventory, confirm a booking, or provide legal certification. Qloo adapter signatures attest to observed responses; they are not Qloo-issued signatures.
- Registered cases are development checks. Independent holdout labels, model comparator measurements, certification, adopter feedback, and business ROI remain `NOT_RUN` or `NOT_MEASURED` until supplied. The acceptance index stays unset without signed independent gates.
- SQLite restore and loopback rollback are local rehearsals. PostgreSQL tests require `OFC_TEST_POSTGRES_URL`; skipped tests do not qualify PostgreSQL. Target-environment restore, deployment, live latency, and production canary results require separate measurements.
- The service has no external-action executor. Human intents and successful policy checks do not authorize payment, booking, publication, or contract execution.
